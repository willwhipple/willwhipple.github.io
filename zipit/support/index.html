My lists aren't syncing between devices

Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple ID on each device and that iCloud is turned on for ZipIt in Settings → [your name] → iCloud. Sync can take a minute, especially on first launch.

How do I back up my data?

In ZipIt, open Settings → Backup & Restore and export a backup file. Save it somewhere safe, like Files or iCloud Drive. You can restore from it on any device.

Does ZipIt collect my data?

No. See the privacy policy.
