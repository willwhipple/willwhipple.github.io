ZipIt Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026

ZipIt is a packing list app. It is built so that your data stays yours. We do not collect, store, or have access to any of your information.

What we collect

Nothing. ZipIt has no accounts and no analytics, advertising, or tracking. It does not send your data to any server we run.

Where your data lives

Your trips, packing lists, and categories are stored on your device. If iCloud is turned on for ZipIt, Apple syncs this data through your own private iCloud account so it appears on your other devices. That data is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. We cannot see it.

Backups

If you export a backup file, it is saved wherever you choose. It goes nowhere else unless you share it.

Children

ZipIt does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
