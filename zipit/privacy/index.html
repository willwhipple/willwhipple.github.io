ZipIt Privacy Policy

Effective 16 November 2026

ZipIt is a packing list app. It's built so that your data stays yours. We don't collect, store or have access to any of your information.

What we collect

Nothing. ZipIt has no accounts and no analytics, advertising or tracking. It doesn't send your data to any server we run.

Where your data lives

Your trips, packing lists, My Stuff and categories are stored on your iPhone. If you turn on iCloud Sync, Apple syncs this data through your own private iCloud account so it appears on your other devices. Apple handles that data under Apple's Privacy Policy. We can't see it.

Apple services ZipIt uses

A few features need Apple's services to work. ZipIt sends them only what that feature needs, and nothing goes to us.

  • Places (Apple Maps). When you search for a trip's destination, what you type is sent to Apple Maps to find the place.
  • Weather (Apple Weather). To show a trip's weather, the destination's location is sent to Apple Weather (not your trip's dates). Weather is kept in a cache on your iPhone and isn't synced to iCloud.
  • Siri. If you use Siri with ZipIt, Apple handles your request. ZipIt makes the change to your list on your iPhone.

These services are run by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. ZipIt doesn't use your device's location.

Your calendar

If you let ZipIt use your calendar, it reads the events around your trips on your iPhone to suggest what to pack and to spot trips from flights and hotels. Your calendar is never sent to us. If you start a trip ZipIt spotted, the place's name or address (never the event itself) is looked up in Apple Maps. You can turn Calendar off in ZipIt's Settings, or remove access in the Settings app.

Suggestions

Packing suggestions are made on your iPhone by Apple Intelligence's on-device model. What they read (your trips, list, weather and calendar) isn't sent anywhere to make them. You can turn Suggestions off in ZipIt's Settings.

Backups

If you export a backup file, it's saved wherever you choose. It goes nowhere else unless you share it.

Deleting your data

Delete ZipIt to remove its data from your iPhone. If iCloud Sync was on, you can also remove ZipIt's iCloud data in the Settings app: tap your name, then iCloud → Manage Storage → ZipIt.

Children

ZipIt doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
