Fixtures Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026

Fixtures shows sports schedules for the teams and events you follow, in your local time, and can add them to your calendar. We do not collect personal information.

What we collect

Nothing personal. Fixtures has no accounts and no analytics, advertising, or tracking.

Schedule data

To show fixtures, the app downloads public sports schedules from our server, which is hosted by Supabase. These requests carry no information about you, your settings, or your calendar. Like any web server, the host may briefly keep standard technical logs, such as IP addresses, for security and reliability. We do not use these logs to identify or track you.

Calendar access

If you choose to add fixtures to your calendar, Fixtures asks for calendar access. It uses that access only to create, update, and remove the fixture events it added, for example when a kick-off time changes. Your calendar data stays on your device and is never sent to us or anyone else. You can turn off access at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Calendars.

Your settings

The teams and sports you follow, your display preferences, and the link between fixtures and calendar events are stored only on your device. They are removed when you delete the app.

Children

Fixtures does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
