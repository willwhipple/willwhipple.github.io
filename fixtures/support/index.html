Fixtures Support

Need help, found a wrong time, or have an idea? Email willwhipple@gmail.com and I'll get back to you.

Which teams and events are covered?

Chelsea FC, Texas Longhorns football, England men's cricket, Formula 1, and the golf majors.

Fixtures aren't being added to my calendar

Check that Fixtures has calendar access in Settings → Privacy & Security → Calendars, and that it's set to Full Access. Full Access lets the app update events when times change.

A time looks wrong or a fixture is missing

Schedules refresh several times a day, and times are shown in your device's time zone. If something still looks off, email me with the fixture and I'll take a look.

Does Fixtures collect my data?

No. See the privacy policy.
